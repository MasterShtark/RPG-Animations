### Bring your character to life.

Wake up in a new world, stretch after a quiet moment, inspect a treasured find and settle beside a campfire. **RPG Animations** adds expressive movement to everyday Minecraft adventures, with animations that nearby players can see too.

**By Shtark · Version 1.0.0**  
**Minecraft 26.3 and 1.21.1 · NeoForge**

## A little more life in every action

- **Wake up and wind down**: cinematic waking, blinking, eye rubbing, climbing into bed and getting back up.
- **Adventure in motion**: walking, sprinting, swimming, climbing, landing, riding and elytra flight.
- **Make every action count**: weapon swings, tool use, fishing, eating, item pickup and dropping.
- **Appreciate your discoveries**: different inspection animations for blocks, tools, weapons and special items.
- **React to the world**: shield your eyes from sunlight and rain, warm your hands, shake off water and react to heat and cold.
- **Express yourself**: wave, nod, bow, beckon, point and give thanks, with idle stretches and typing gestures.
- **Everyday details**: equipment adjustments, workstation gestures and distinct container interactions.
- **Share the moment**: multiplayer animations and optional inventory, book and backpack previews.

## Install

Choose the JAR matching your Minecraft version:

| Minecraft | NeoForge | Java | Mod version |
|---|---|---|---|
| 26.3 | 26.3.0.76 | 25 | 1.0.0 |
| 1.21.1 | 21.1.259 | 21 | 1.0.0 |

Place the matching JAR in your instance's `mods` folder. Use the same build on the server and all participating clients. Singleplayer works too.

The [26.3 source](https://github.com/MasterShtark/RPG-Animations/tree/main/src) is at the repository root. The [1.21.1 source](versions/1.21.1) has its own build and configuration. Each version produces its own JAR.

## Make it yours

Open **Mods > RPG Animations > Config** to toggle individual animations, enable reduced motion and choose animation priorities. Per-action settings let Punchy and Better Combat handle overlapping actions while RPG animations remain available for others.

Inventory and book previews are **opt-in**: enabling sharing lets nearby players see those contents. Chat messages and desktop images are not shared.

| Command | Action |
|---|---|
| `/rpganimation menu` | Open the gesture menu |
| `/rpganimation preview` | Replay the wake-up animation |
| `/rpganimation skip` | Stop the wake-up animation |
